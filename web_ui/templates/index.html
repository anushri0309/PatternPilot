Print at 100% scale

  1. Press Print pattern (or open the downloaded SVG in your browser).
  2. In the print dialog choose Scale: 100% and turn off “Fit to page”.
  3. Pick a paper size that fits (see “Real size” above), or print on several sheets and tape them together.
  4. Measure a printed dimension arrow with a ruler to confirm the scale.
How it works
1Photo
2AI reads the garment
3Your measurements
4Pattern drafted
5Printable SVG

Pattern Pilot keeps seeing and sewing separate. First, an AI model looks at your photo and predicts details such as garment type, silhouette, neckline and sleeve length. Those predictions are only a starting point, so you can correct them.

Then a mathematical drafting engine, based on Winifred Aldrich’s pattern-drafting method, combines your choices with your body measurements to calculate the exact shape of each pattern piece. The result is drawn as an SVG with stitching lines, cutting lines, notches and grainlines.

Because of that split, a surprising pattern can come from a wrong prediction, a measurement typo, or the drafting step. Reviewing steps 2 and 3 carefully gives the best result.
